# kimishinu-onigiri-bridge
> *A tiny bridge for keeping up with everything KimiShinu.*

**kimishinu-onigiri-bridge** is a fan-made project that monitors multiple sources for new updates related to *Kimi ga Shinu made Koi o Shitai* (KimiShinu), collects relevant information, and delivers it to a private channel.
The idea is simple: rather than having the author wander from site to site and manually sift through updates, the bridge brings everything important together in one place.

## What It Does
- Fetches the latest KimiShinu-related updates from multiple sources.
- Focuses exclusively on information relevant to KimiShinu.
- Normalizes and deduplicates incoming updates.
- Delivers useful notifications to a private channel.
- Saves the author from repeatedly checking and skimming through multiple platforms.
Potential sources include Twitter/X, MangaDex, YouTube, and relevant storefronts. The exact list may expand as development progresses.

## Development Status
This project is currently under development. Its architecture, features, and overall behavior may change as the bridge is built, tested, and refined.
It is not intended to be presented as a finished or production-ready service at this stage.

## Why It Exists

This is a small passion project, made purely for fun and out of love for KimiShinu.

Its purpose is simply to make following new information a little easier and more enjoyable - not to turn that enthusiasm into a business.
